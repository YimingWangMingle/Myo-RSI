# Limits of the v0.1r evidence

- The unit of evaluation is one seeded target episode. Each bank saw the same 100 targets. The three NMF initializations reuse both the expert data and targets. Combining them into a nominal sample of 300 independent targets would understate uncertainty. The 95% Wilson intervals for the two teacher policies refer only to their local validation samples.
- The official release tag is v3.1.0, and the teacher manifests were generated with MyoSuite 3.0.0. Local contracts, normalizers and behavioral gates were checked. The upstream table uses different targets. Training cost, upstream randomness and exact physical equivalence across releases remain uncertain.
- The fresh native CPU/GPU probes checked action mapping and task contracts. Floating-point dynamics can vary across GPU runs. Weighted reward terms at the simulator step supply the terminal error before any reset. Exact artifact checksums identify this run.
- The dataset contains 941 successful, 55 near-success and four other failure episodes. All four larger failures fell in the fit partition. Generalization to severe off-distribution control errors is poorly constrained. The phase cap balances within-episode time and gives each target the same fitted frame budget. The eight spatial bins are a coverage diagnostic.
- Nearest physical-action projection is a surrogate for task performance. The clipped nonlinear objective admits local optima. At high K the primary solver often reached its cap. A separate double-precision solver substantially improved stationarity and reached at most 27/100. Two NMF fits reached 600 iterations. [Solver diagnostics](solver_sensitivity.csv) · [Fit diagnostics](bank_reconstruction.csv)
- The experiment used a single Reach teacher and one basis-learning objective. It does not estimate performance of an optimally trained latent policy, identify physiological neural synergies, or test an RSI agent. The gate left T1/T2 downstream PPO, BC, final tests and T3 adaptation unmeasured.
- The reserved final seeds also appeared in the earlier v0.1 release. They were never instantiated in this rerun. An independent confirmatory study would require a newly registered cohort.
- Wall times are host measurements. GPU kernel-active time, download time, and total external teacher-training cost are unmeasured. [Cost ledger](resource_ledger.csv)
