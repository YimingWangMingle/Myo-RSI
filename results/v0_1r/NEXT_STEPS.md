# Experiments motivated by the P2 stop

1. Use the saved paired episodes to localize the first divergence in fingertip position and muscle recruitment. Compare the larger losses on feedback-induced states with smaller one-step errors at the original reset states. A follow-up can stratify by target and movement phase.
2. In a separately registered development experiment, add an identity-action positive control and sweep larger K toward 39. This can distinguish a dimensionality limit from a limitation of the current decomposition and projection. Match seeds, control constraints and solver diagnostics.
3. Compare action-only NMF with a task-sensitive basis using distinct training targets. Freeze data, candidate list, selection rule and an independent confirmatory cohort before interpreting success. Check effects on failure-state coverage and per-target uncertainty.
4. If a basis passes closed-loop verification, resume the long direct pilot and the matched three-seed T1/T2 comparison. Give both action parameterizations the same demonstrations and PPO budget before making comparative efficiency claims. T3 requires a competent source policy.
